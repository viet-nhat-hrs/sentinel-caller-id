FAQ
Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-in or registration. Open the app and start using it.

Why does Sentinel ask to be my default phone app?

The call screen and call screening work through Android’s phone-app role. You can choose this in a system dialog and change it at any time in Android Settings.

What does Unseen need?

Unseen is optional and uses Notification access, which you grant yourself in system settings. It reads message previews from supported chat apps and keeps them on your device.

Does it upload my call history or contacts?

Call history, contacts and message previews are used on your device to provide the features. Sentinel has no account and does not operate a server that receives them. See the Privacy Policy for details, including the advertising and analytics services the app includes.

Will it block every spam call?

No app can. Spam risk is checked against an on-device database of reported numbers, and you decide what to block.

Which languages are supported?

15 languages, including English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Hindi, Arabic and Vietnamese.
